MECHANICS
ROLLING
The monolith does not slide. Every move is a quarter-turn roll around whichever edge faces the direction you press.

When standing upright it is 1×1×2. Roll it east or west and it lands 2×1×1 lying along the east-west axis. Roll it again in the same direction and it stands again — one step forward.
Plan several moves ahead. The orientation after each roll determines which moves are even possible.
OBJECTIVE
THE RIFT
Each level contains one rift — a glowing portal slab. To clear the level you must stop the monolith on the rift while it is standing upright (1×1 footprint).

Arriving on the rift while lying flat does nothing. You must approach from a position where the final roll lands you standing.
The rift counts moves and tracks your best. Fewer moves is better.
HAZARDS
CRACKED SLABS
Cracked slabs glow with an amber fracture pattern. They cannot bear the full upright weight of the monolith — if you end a move standing on one, it shatters and you fall.

Rolling across a cracked slab while lying flat spreads the load and is safe. Use that to your advantage.
Falling resets the level from the start position. Your move count continues — restarts are free with R.
MECHANICS
SWITCHES
Switches extend light-bridges across the void. Roll over one — or stand on one — to toggle the bridges in its group.

Soft switches fire from any orientation. Heavy switches require the full upright weight of the block standing on them — rolling across flat does nothing.
Bridges can be toggled on and off. Some puzzles require you to manage bridge state carefully.
